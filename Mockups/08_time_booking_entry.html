❄ Time Booking Posting

The core transaction. Book technician time against a Work Order at Task level. The system auto-fills Customer, Equipment, Industry & Location from the Job, fetches each Task's Standard Hours, classifies Normal/Overtime, picks the rate and computes labour cost. Each booking line is keyed by Job No + Task.
